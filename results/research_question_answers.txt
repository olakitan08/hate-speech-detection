  Interpretation:
  - If DL consistently outperforms ML on HateXplain but not Davidson,
    the architecture + contextual embeddings advantage is dataset-conditional.
  - If the gap holds on both datasets at large scale, architecture and
    feature representation together drive performance.
  - If ML achieves competitive results at small sizes on Davidson,
    TF-IDF feature quality is the key determinant for explicit content.
